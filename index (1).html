How VIVAAN Works: 6 Steps

A seamless journey from farm verification to buyer doorstep delivery

1
Verify
Identity KYC, agricultural land documents (Khasra/Khatauni), and bank account verification create a trusted Digital Farmer ID.
2
List
Farmers list fresh harvested lots: crop variety, harvest date, available weight, grade photos, and transparent baseline rate per kg.
3
Discover
Buyers explore certified farm inventories by distance, organic certification, harvest freshness, and immediate dispatch availability.
4
Multi-Cart Order
Combine lots from multiple local farmers into one consolidated basket. Unified escrow payment processed securely via Razorpay.
5
Deliver
VIVAAN's logistics engine batches suitable multi-farm pickups into one optimized vehicle route, reducing shipping expense significantly.
6
Complete & Settle
Driver delivers produce to buyer doorstep. On 4-digit OTP entry, Razorpay escrow instantly unlocks and credits both farmer and driver accounts.
